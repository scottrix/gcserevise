# GCSE Revise

Free GCSE revision notes for 37 subjects across all major UK exam boards. No login, no data collection, no paywall.

## Subjects

All major GCSE subjects covered including:

- **Core**: English Language, English Literature, Mathematics
- **Science**: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Combined Science, Computer Science, Astronomy, Geology, Electronics
- **Humanities**: History, Geography, Religious Studies, Citizenship Studies, Classical Civilisation, Ancient History
- **Languages**: French, Spanish, German, Latin
- **Creative**: Art & Design, Music, Drama, Dance, Media Studies, Film Studies
- **Technical**: Design & Technology, Food Preparation & Nutrition, Engineering
- **Social Sciences**: Business Studies, Economics, Psychology, Sociology, Law, Physical Education, Statistics

## Exam Boards

- AQA
- Edexcel (Pearson)
- OCR
- Eduqas (WJEC)
- CCEA

## Features

- **1,200+ topic pages** — comprehensive coverage across all subjects
- **Practice questions** — with detailed answers and exam tips
- **Required practicals** — for science subjects
- **Dark/light mode** — easy on eyes for late-night revision
- **Mobile friendly** — revise anywhere on your phone
- **No login required** — no accounts, no data collection
- **Free forever** — no paywalls, no premium tiers
