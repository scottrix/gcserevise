🎯 Coursework & NEA Guide

How it is assessed (AQA 8552; other boards similar — check): written exam ~50% (core technical principles, specialist knowledge, designing) plus a design-and-make NEA ~50% responding to a contextual challenge released in June of Year 10.

Your NEA frame — folder structure (roughly 20–30 pages):

  • Context analysis + client/user interview (named user needs)
  • Design brief + measurable specification (each point testable)
  • Research: existing products, materials, anthropometrics — each page ending in a design decision
  • Initial ideas (annotated sketches) → development (models, testing, iteration evidence with dates and photos)
  • Final prototype: manufacturing plan, cutting list, quality-control checks
  • Evaluation against EVERY specification point + client feedback

Top pitfalls: undated evidence (iteration cannot be seen); no real user feedback; specification points that cannot be tested; skipping modelling evidence because 'it looked messy' — messy models are marks.
