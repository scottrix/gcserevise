🎯 Coursework & NEA Guide

How it is assessed (AQA 8261; other boards similar — check): written exam ~40% (set play + live theatre evaluation) · devising ~40% (devised performance + portfolio) · text performance ~20% (two contrasting extracts).

Your NEA frame — devising portfolio structure:

  • Section 1: response to the stimulus — why this idea, what you researched
  • Section 2: development — rehearsal decisions with DATES, what you tried, what you cut, and WHY (mark schemes reward reasoned change)
  • Section 3: your individual contribution — character/notebook evidence, specific vocal/physical choices with references to practitioner techniques
  • Section 4: evaluation — what worked live, what you would revisit, audience impact

Live theatre review prep: at the production you see, note THREE specific design moments (lighting/sound/set) and TWO acting moments with exact descriptions — reviews earn marks by quoting what happened on stage, not by general praise.

Top pitfalls: a portfolio written like a diary (no analysis); extracts from the same scene for both performance pieces; no documented rehearsal changes.
