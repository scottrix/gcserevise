Eduqas GCSE Geology. Assessment runs through written papers examining geological principles (minerals, rocks, fossils, structures) and geological Britain/processes, with map- and fieldwork-style questions inside the papers rather than separate coursework. These pages cover the rock cycle, plate tectonics, geological time, applied and economic geology topics — check the current paper structure and fieldwork expectations for your exam year.
