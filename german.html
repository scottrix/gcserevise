🧭 Board Differences

AQA

Unitised assessment (Northern Ireland). Listening, reading and writing are examined; the speaking test is teacher-conducted and externally moderated. CCEA writing tasks are shorter but more frequent — build accuracy in short pieces, especially present/past/future verb frames.

Pearson Edexcel

Unitised assessment (Northern Ireland). Listening, reading and writing are examined; the speaking test is teacher-conducted and externally moderated. CCEA writing tasks are shorter but more frequent — build accuracy in short pieces, especially present/past/future verb frames.

Eduqas
