📋 How to Use Past Papers Effectively

Tip: Start with untimed practice using your notes, then progress to timed conditions as you gain confidence.
  1. Start early - Don't leave past papers until the last minute
  2. Use mark schemes - Mark your own work honestly to identify gaps
  3. Track your scores - Keep a log of your scores to track progress
  4. Focus on weak areas - Spend more time on topics where you lose marks
  5. Simulate exam conditions - Practice under timed conditions without notes
  6. Review examiner reports - Learn from common mistakes other students make
