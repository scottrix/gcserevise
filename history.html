📜 GCSE History

Foundation Higher All Boards

Complete revision notes for GCSE History covering 32 topics across 4 strands. Focused on the most popular AQA options: Germany 1890-1945, Inter-War Years, Health and the People, and Elizabethan England. Also relevant to Edexcel and OCR specifications.

📊 Course Overview

GCSE History is assessed through two written papers. Paper 1 focuses on understanding the modern world; Paper 2 focuses on shaping the nation. Both papers are worth 50% of the overall grade.
