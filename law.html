AQA GCSE Law (4160) — status check. AQA withdrew its GCSE Law specification and no awarding body currently offers GCSE Law in England; final awards were taken a number of years ago (confirm the exact position with your centre). These pages remain valuable as a foundation for A-Level Law and for citizenship/legal-literacy study: the English legal system, courts and personnel, and law in action (criminal, tort and family topics). Do not enter for a GCSE Law exam without first confirming availability with an awarding body.
