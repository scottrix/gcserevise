Foundation Higher All Boards

Complete revision notes for GCSE Physics (Triple Science) covering all 30 topics across 7 strands. Aligned with AQA, Edexcel, OCR and CCEA specifications. Includes content beyond Combined Science.

📊 Course Overview

GCSE Physics (Triple Science) covers all Combined Science Physics content in greater depth, plus additional topics including Space Physics. Foundation tier covers grades 1-5; Higher tier covers grades 4-9. 10 required practicals for AQA.
