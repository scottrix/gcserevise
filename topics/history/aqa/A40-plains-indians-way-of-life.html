H1: Kaiser Wilhelm and Germany

Foundation Higher AQAEdexcelOCREduqasCCEA

Lakota bands and tribes, the buffalo economy, counting coup and beliefs, the horse — with structure vocabulary for way-of-life questions.

📋 Key Concepts

Structure: bands (extended family, ~10–30 tipis) formed tribes (Lakota, Cheyenne) only for summer hunts; chiefs led by consent; women owned the tipi and processed buffalo; old age = wisdom. Beliefs: Wakan Tanka (Great Spirit), the sacred circle, land held in common (never owned), visions and the Sun Dance. Buffalo = life: food, tipi, clothing, tools, fuel, ceremony. Warfare: small-scale and ritual — counting coup (touching an enemy) outranked killing. Horses (1600s+): transformed hunting, warfare and wealth.

🧮 Worked Examples — Exam Style

Describe two features of Plains Indian beliefs (4).

« They believed in Wakan Tanka, the Great Spirit present in all things. The circle was sacred — tipis, camps and the Sun Dance were circular because life had no end. »

❓ Practice Questions

Q1: Name two roles of women in Plains bands.

Q2: Describe two features of the Sun Dance.

Q3: What was counting coup, and why did it matter?

Q4: Describe two uses of the buffalo.

Q5: 'The horse was the most important change in Plains life.' How far do you agree? (16 + 4)

✅ Answers

  1. Women: owned the tipi, butchered and processed buffalo, influenced band decisions.
  2. Sun Dance: summer renewal ceremony — later banned by the US government.
  3. Counting coup: touching an enemy with a coup stick earned the highest honour.
  4. Buffalo: food, tipi covering, clothing, shields, fuel (dung), ceremony.
  5. Land: held in common and sacred — US ownership law violated the Plains way.

🗺️ In the Exam

['Homestead Act 1862: 160 acres, 5 years; railroad 1869; barbed wire 1874.', 'Band (10-30 tipis) -> tribe; chiefs by consent; women owned the tipi.', 'Counting coup outranked killing; buffalo = economy + religion; land held in common.']

⚠️ Common Errors

  • Wrong: Plains people were 'primitive' Correct: Their society solved survival on the Plains elegantly — mobility, consent and ecology in balance
  • Wrong: Chiefs ordered everyone about Correct: Leadership was by consent; war chiefs led only raids

🧭 Board Focus — AQA

Spec 8145. Paper 1: Understanding the modern world (one period study + one wider-world depth study). Paper 2: Shaping the nation (one thematic study with historic environment + one British depth study including the historic environment). AQA's AO4 historic-environment questions reward site-specific knowledge, not general period knowledge.

Always check: specification details change — confirm paper structure and set texts against the current specification for your exam year.

  • Learn band → tribe structure with ONE example (Lakota).
  • Buffalo + horse = your two evidence anchors for any way-of-life question.
