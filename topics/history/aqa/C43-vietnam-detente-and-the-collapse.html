H1: Kaiser Wilhelm and Germany

Foundation Higher AQAEdexcelOCREduqasCCEA

Vietnam's cost to US credibility, SALT and Helsinki, the Afghan invasion, Reagan and SDI, Gorbachev's choices, the fall of the Wall and the USSR's dissolution.

📋 Key Concepts

Vietnam (1954–75): domino theory; Gulf of Tonkin (1964) → ~540,000 troops (1969); Tet (1968) turned opinion; withdrawal (1973), Saigon fell (1975). Détente: SALT I (1972), Helsinki (1975); ended by the Afghan invasion (Dec 1979) → SALT II shelved, boycotts (1980/84), Reagan’s build-up (‘evil empire’, SDI 1983). Gorbachev: glasnost, perestroika, INF Treaty (Dec 1987) — first to DELETE a warhead class; unilateral cuts; 1989: Poland June, Wall 9 Nov; 1991: Warsaw Pact dissolved, failed coup, USSR dissolved (25 December).

🧮 Worked Examples — Exam Style

Pact dissolved, failed coup, USSR dissolved (25 December).

✅ Answers

  1. Describe two features of the Tet Offensive.
  2. Give two features of détente (SALT I or Helsinki).
  3. Describe two features of the Soviet-Afghan war.
  4. Explain why the Berlin Wall fell in 1989 (two reasons, 4 marks).
  5. 'Reagan's SDI won the Cold War.' How far do you agree? (16 + 4)

📌 Key Facts to Memorise

  • Gulf of Tonkin 1964; Tet 1968; Paris Accords 1973; Saigon 1975.
  • SALT I 1972; Helsinki 1975; Afghanistan Dec 1979; SDI 1983; INF Dec 1987.
  • 1989: Poland June, Wall 9 Nov; 1991: coup Aug, dissolution 25 Dec.
  • Gorbachev: glasnost (openness), perestroika (restructuring).

🗺️ In the Exam

End-of-Cold-War essays layer causes: long (economy), short (Gorbachev’s choices), immediate (1989 revolutions). Afghanistan = ‘Soviet Vietnam’ — the analogy writes itself.

⚠️ Common Errors

  • Wrong: The USSR collapsed militarily Correct: The army was intact — the economy and legitimacy collapsed
  • Wrong: Reagan forced every concession Correct: Gorbachev outbid his own generals; many cuts were unilateral

🧭 Board Focus — AQA

Spec 8145. Paper 1: Understanding the modern world (one period study + one wider-world depth study). Paper 2: Shaping the nation (one thematic study with historic environment + one British depth study including the historic environment). AQA's AO4 historic-environment questions reward site-specific knowledge, not general period knowledge.

Always check: specification details change — confirm paper structure and set texts against the current specification for your exam year.

🎯 Exam Tips

  • The 1961 Wall vs the 1989 Wall speech = a ready-made change-over-time pair.
  • INF 1987 = first DELETED class — contrast with SALT’s limits.
