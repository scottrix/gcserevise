H1: Kaiser Wilhelm and Germany

Foundation Higher AQAEdexcelOCREduqasCCEA

Nicholas II's autocracy, opposition parties, industrialisation's strains, Bloody Sunday 1905, the October Manifesto and Stolypin's Dumas.

📋 Key Concepts

Autocracy: Nicholas II (1894–1917) ruled by divine right; censorship; Okhrana. Opposition: Kadets (liberals), Social Revolutionaries (peasants, terror), Social Democrats (Marxists — Bolshevik/Menshevik split 1903). Strains: Witte’s industrialisation, peasant land hunger, defeat in the Russo-Japanese War (1904–05). 1905: Bloody Sunday (22 January) — Gapon’s peaceful march shot down; strikes, Potemkin mutiny, peasant disorders → the October Manifesto (liberties + Duma) split the opposition; the Fundamental Laws (1906) restored autocracy; Stolypin repressed (the “necktie”) and reformed (wager on the strong); the 1907 electoral coup packed the third Duma.

🧮 Worked Examples — Exam Style

'Bloody Sunday was the main cause of the 1905 Revolution.' How far do you agree? (16 + 4)

YES: it destroyed the Tsar’s 'Little Father' image and triggered the national strike wave. NO: industrial misery, land hunger, military defeat and liberal demands predated it. Verdict: Bloody Sunday was the TRIGGER; structural grievances were the CAUSE — the distinction earns top bands.

❓ Practice Questions

Q1: Describe two features of Bloody Sunday (1905).

Q2: Give two features of the October Manifesto.

Q3: Describe two features of opposition to the Tsar before 1905.

Q4: Explain why the 1905 Revolution failed (two reasons, 4 marks).

Q5: 'The October Manifesto saved the Tsar in 1905.' How far do you agree? (16 + 4)

✅ Answers

  1. Bloody Sunday 22 Jan 1905 (Gapon).
  2. October Manifesto Oct 1905; Fundamental Laws Apr 1906 ('supreme autocratic power').
  3. Stolypin: wager on the strong, hangings, 1907 franchise change.
  4. Dumas 1 and 2 dissolved; third (1907) loyal.

📌 Key Facts to Memorise

  • Sand Creek 1864; Fetterman 1866; Treaty 1868; Little Bighorn 1876; Wounded Knee 1890.
  • Dawes Act 1887: 160-acre allotments, 'surplus' sold.
  • Buffalo ~30 million to near-extinction by 1885.

🗺️ In the Exam

1905 vs 1917 comparisons: 1905 = spontaneous, loyal, ended by concessions; 1917 = organised, war-worn, irreversible. Keep the comparison ready.

⚠️ Common Errors

  • Wrong: The Duma had real power from 1906 Correct: The Fundamental Laws kept autocracy — the Tsar ruled by decree
  • Wrong: Bloody Sunday was a revolutionary plot Correct: Gapon’s march was peaceful — the shooting radicalised it

🧭 Board Focus — AQA

Spec 8145. Paper 1: Understanding the modern world (one period study + one wider-world depth study). Paper 2: Shaping the nation (one thematic study with historic environment + one British depth study including the historic environment). AQA's AO4 historic-environment questions reward site-specific knowledge, not general period knowledge.

Always check: specification details change — confirm paper structure and set texts against the current specification for your exam year.

🎯 Exam Tips

  • Trigger-vs-cause distinction for every revolution question.
  • 1905 = dress rehearsal — use it to explain February 1917’s success.
