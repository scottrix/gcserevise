H1: Kaiser Wilhelm and Germany

Foundation Higher AQAEdexcelOCREduqasCCEA

Reds vs Whites 1918–21, Trotsky's Red Army, War Communism, the Red Terror, Kronstadt 1921, famine, and Lenin's New Economic Policy.

📋 Key Concepts

Civil War (1918–21): Reds (centre, railways, industry) vs Whites (divided peripheries, foreign intervention) vs Greens (peasant armies). Trotsky’s Red Army: conscription, ex-Tsarist officers under commissars, death penalty, armoured trains. War Communism: grain requisitioning, nationalisation, banned trade → famine 1921–22 (~5m dead); Kronstadt rising (March 1921) — ‘Soviets without Bolsheviks’ — crushed. NEP (1921): tax in kind, private trade legalised, state kept the ‘commanding heights’; Scissors Crisis 1923; Lenin’s strokes 1922–24 → the succession.

🧮 Worked Examples — Exam Style

'The Reds won the Civil War because of Trotsky's leadership.' How far do you agree? (16 + 4)

YES: built a ~5-million-man army, used ex-officers with commissars, defended the rail hub triangle. NO: Whites were geographically split and politically divided; peasants feared landowner restoration; Red Terror secured the rear; intervention was half-hearted. Verdict: Trotsky organised victory; geography and White disunity gifted it.

❓ Practice Questions

Q1: Describe two features of War Communism.

Q2: Give two reasons the Whites lost the Civil War.

Q3: What happened at Kronstadt (1921)?

Q4: Explain why Lenin introduced the NEP (two reasons, 4 marks).

Q5: 'War Communism was necessary to win the war.' How far do you agree? (16 + 4)

✅ Answers

  1. Civil War 1918–21; Red Army ~5m; intervention by Britain, France, USA, Japan.
  2. War Communism: requisitioning, nationalisation, banned private trade.
  3. Kronstadt March 1921; NEP: tax in kind, NEPmen, kulaks; Scissors Crisis 1923.
  4. Famine 1921–22 (~5m deaths).

📌 Key Facts to Memorise

  • Abdication 2 Mar 1917; dual power; Order No. 1.
  • April Theses Apr 1917; July Days; Kornilov Aug; seizure 24-25 Oct; Sovnarkom.
  • PG failures: war, land, postponed elections.

🗺️ In the Exam

NEP = ideological retreat, economic recovery — the classic two-sided verdict. Reds-vs-Whites needs geography: the Red industrial centre vs four scattered White fronts.

⚡ Targeted Drills

  • White fronts: Kolchak (E), Denikin (S), Yudenich (NW), Miller (N) — never linked.
  • Trotsky: ex-officers + commissars; decimation; armoured trains.
  • War Communism: prodrazvyorstka; ban on private trade; nationalisation.
  • Famine 1921–22 (~5m); American Relief Administration fed millions.
  • Kronstadt Mar 1921 → NEP 10th Party Congress (same month).

📖 Detailed Notes: Why the Reds Won — Factor by Factor

  • Geography: the Red core held Petrograd, Moscow and the railway spoke — interior lines; the Whites fought on four peripheral fronts (Kolchak from Siberia, Denikin from the south, Yudenich from Estonia, Miller from the north) that never linked.
  • Organisation: Trotsky’s armoured-train command tour; 50,000 ex-Tsarist officers (military specialists) watched by commissars; hostages and decimation enforced discipline; the Red Army grew from ~100,000 to 5,000,000.
  • Politics: the Whites stood for restoring a broken past (landowners would return land); the Reds defended the peasants’ land seizure — even peasants who hated requisitioning feared White victory more; national minorities feared White "Russia One and Indivisible".
  • Terror as policy: the Cheka (Dzerzhinsky); Red Terror declared Sept 1918 (after Uritsky and the Lenin shooting); 50,000+ shot; class-hostage system. War Communism fed cities by starving villages (prodrazvyorstka took "surplus", really seed grain).
  • Aftermath: ~10m dead (war + famine + terror); Kronstadt (March 1921) — the sailors of 1917 rose for "Soviets without Bolsheviks"; Tukhachevsky stormed the ice fortress; the NEP followed within days of the final suppression.

⚠️ Common Errors

  • Wrong: The NEP was Stalin's policy Correct: Lenin’s 1921 retreat — Stalin ended it with collectivisation in 1929
  • Wrong: The Whites were all Tsarists Correct: They included SRs and liberals — unity only in anti-Bolshevism

🧭 Board Focus — AQA

Spec 8145. Paper 1: Understanding the modern world (one period study + one wider-world depth study). Paper 2: Shaping the nation (one thematic study with historic environment + one British depth study including the historic environment). AQA's AO4 historic-environment questions reward site-specific knowledge, not general period knowledge.

✍️ Extended Answer Plan — "The NEP betrayed the Revolution." How far do you agree? (16 + 4)

BETRAYAL: private trade and NEPmen restored capitalism; kulaks grew rich; socialist ideals abandoned; Bukharin’s "enrich yourselves" scandalised purists.

SAVED IT: production recovered to 1913 levels by 1926; famine ended; the party survived (Kronstadt proved the alternative was collapse); Lenin called it a "retreat to take two steps back for one forward".

Judgement: the NEP saved the revolution’s STATE while betraying its ECONOMY — and the party never forgave it (Stalin ended it in 1929). Conclude: betrayal of theory, loyalty to survival — argue the party chose survival, proving what it really valued.

🔬 Case Study Focus — The Kronstadt Rebellion (March 1921)

The sailors of Kronstadt — the fortress island guarding Petrograd, heroes of 1917 ("the reddest of the red") — rose against War Communism: for re-elected soviets, free speech for socialists, release of political prisoners, an end to requisitioning. Tukhachevsky stormed the ice fortress with 60,000 troops across frozen Gulf ice (7–18 March); ~10,000 casualties; survivors shot or sent to the first gulags. Lenin called it "the flash that lit up reality better than anything else" — the NEP was announced days later at the same Party Congress. Exam use: Kronstadt proves War Communism had broken the regime’s own base — the most persuasive single piece of evidence for any "War Communism failed" essay.

🎯 Exam Tips

  • Kronstadt = the loyalists’ turn — quote ‘Soviets without Bolsheviks’.
  • Civil War map argument: Red centre vs White peripheries.
